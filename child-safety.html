Marilo Child Safety Standards

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Marilo has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation, child sexual abuse material, grooming, or any behavior that endangers children.

Application: Marilo
Package name: com.mkayaaslan.rallyo
Developer: Mehmet Kayaaslan

Prohibited Content and Conduct

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Child sexual exploitation
  • Grooming or predatory behavior
  • Sexualized content involving minors
  • Any activity that exploits or endangers children

Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report suspected child exploitation, abuse, or unsafe behavior by contacting us at mehmetkayaaslan@outlook.com.

  1. Use the subject line: Marilo Child Safety Report
  2. Include the username or group involved, and the approximate date and time.
  3. Include any relevant details that may help us review the report.
Do not send, attach, or reproduce illegal material in your report. Describe what you saw instead. If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

Enforcement

When we identify content or behavior that violates these standards, we may remove content, restrict or terminate accounts, preserve relevant records, and cooperate with competent authorities where required by law.

Compliance

Marilo complies with applicable child safety laws and Google Play's Child Safety Standards requirements.
