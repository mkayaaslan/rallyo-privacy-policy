Groups and location sharing

How do I join a group?

A group admin adds you from their friends list. Send the admin a friend request first; once they accept, they can add you to the group. Marilo has no invite links on purpose: a link can be passed on, and anyone holding it would see where the group is.

Why do other members not see me on the map?

Location sharing is switched on per group. Open the group, go to Group Info and turn location sharing on. Marilo also needs location permission: on iPhone, Settings → Marilo → Location → While Using the App.

Does sharing continue when the app is in the background?

Yes, while a group map session is active. On iPhone you will see the blue location indicator at the top of the screen. Closing the app from the app switcher, or turning sharing off in Group Info, stops it.

How much battery does it use?

Marilo adapts how often it reads GPS to how you move: more often while you walk or drive, rarely while you stand still. Low Power Mode and a weak GPS signal (indoors, deep valleys) can make updates less frequent.

I am not getting notifications.

Check Settings → Marilo → Notifications and make sure they are allowed. While a group's chat or map is open on your screen, its messages are not shown as notifications, because you are already looking at them.

Subscription

How do I cancel Marilo Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by your app store. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Marilo Pro. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

I bought Pro but the app still shows the free plan.

Open your Profile and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same account and the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. If it still does not work, write to us with the purchase date.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you paid: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google Play from your Play order history.

Account and safety

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Delete Account. This removes your account, profile, friendships, group memberships and location data. More details: Account Deletion. If you have an active subscription, cancel it in your store as well; deleting the account does not stop store billing.

How do I report or block someone?

Press and hold a chat message to report it, or open the person's profile and choose Report User or Block user. We review every report as soon as possible (see the Terms of Service, section 8). You can also write to the address above.
